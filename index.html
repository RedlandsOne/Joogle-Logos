Thinking behind it

The eyes

The double “oo” become a pair of googly eyes that actually look around. They make the name memorable and hint at what a search engine does: look things up for you.

The strokes

Every letter is drawn with one thick stroke and round ends, so the mark stays friendly, scales cleanly and has no font to license.

The colour

Warm, sun-faded primaries on cream. The colours feel familiar for a search brand, but they're Joogle's own set: tomato, mustard, teal and cobalt, with navy for type.
